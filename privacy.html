Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-21

MLBB Video Generator ("the App") is a desktop application that helps users create and publish short-form videos to TikTok.

Information We Process

  • TikTok authorization data: access tokens/credentials you provide or authorize, used only to perform TikTok actions you request, such as publishing content.
  • Content you choose to publish: video files, captions, hashtags, and privacy settings.
  • Local technical data: basic logs or local configuration stored on your device for troubleshooting and app operation.

How We Use Information

We use information only to operate the App, authenticate with TikTok APIs when authorized, publish or upload content at your request, and troubleshoot issues.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Information is shared only with TikTok through official TikTok APIs as needed to provide the requested functionality, or when required by law.

Data Storage and Security

The App is primarily local/desktop software. Tokens and configuration may be stored locally on your device. You are responsible for securing access to your device and local files.

Your Choices

You can revoke the App's TikTok access in your TikTok account settings and delete local tokens/data from your device at any time.
